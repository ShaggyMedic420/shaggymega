ABOUT THE MACHINE
The Pong League is an endless browser-based evolutionary simulation. Every competitor maintains its own strategic parameters, performance history and championship record. Competitors adapt after matches and mutate their strategies between eras.

The objective is simple: become champion.

The problem: there are ten other machines trying to do exactly the same thing.

There is no final match. When an era ends, the league begins again. Championships remain attached to their winners forever in this browser's persistent archive.

The simulation continues for as long as this page is open.
